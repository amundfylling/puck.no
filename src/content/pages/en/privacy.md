The Norwegian Table Hockey Association (NBHF) processes personal data in connection with its activities, including tournament administration, membership records and operation of the website [puck.no](https://www.puck.no).

## 1. Data Controller

The Norwegian Table Hockey Association (NBHF) board is the data controller for the processing of personal data on the website and in connection with association events.

* **Privacy contact:** Amund Risa Fylling
* **Email:** [amund.fylling@puck.no](mailto:amund.fylling@puck.no)

## 2. Personal Data Collected

When registering for tournaments and using the website, the following information may be collected:

* **Player Identity & Ranking:** Name, club, country and ITHF player ID (from the International Table Hockey Federation world ranking).
* **Contact Information:** Email address and phone number provided upon registration.
* **Registration Questions:** Answers to specific tournament questions (such as meal choices or category selection).

## 3. Public vs. Private Information

We strictly distinguish between public participant information and private contact details:

* **Public Information:** Name, club, country, ITHF player ID, tournament results and placements are displayed in public participant lists and result tables.
* **Private Information:** Email address, phone number and custom registration answers are kept **confidential** and are **never** exposed in public participant lists or to unauthorized parties.

## 4. Purposes and Legal Basis

Personal data is processed for the following purposes:

1. **Tournament Administration:** To process registrations, seed matches, draw groups and publish results.
   * *Legal Basis:* Contract with participant (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and legitimate interest in organizing sports tournaments (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
2. **Historical Results & Statistics:** Publication of tournament results and ITHF ranking points.
   * *Legal Basis:* Legitimate interest in maintaining sports statistics and historical archives (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
3. **Communication:** Sending practical event information prior to tournaments when necessary.

## 5. Service Providers & Processors

We use reliable technology providers to deliver secure digital services:

* **Cloudflare Pages & Cloudflare D1:** Website hosting, API functions and tournament registration storage in the Cloudflare D1 database.
* **Cloudflare Turnstile:** Protection of registration forms against automated spam. Turnstile processes technical network data without setting tracking cookies.
* **GitHub:** Source code management, website builds and CMS content storage.
* **ITHF (International Table Hockey Federation):** Retrieval of world ranking data and submission of international ranking points.
* **Cloudflare Web Analytics:** Privacy-friendly website analytics without cookies or cross-site tracking.

## 6. Retention Period

* Email addresses and phone numbers are stored only for as long as necessary to manage the specific tournament and seasonal wrap-up.
* Names and tournament results are retained in historical competition archives.

## 7. Information Regarding Minors

Minor participants (under 18 years of age) are welcome in tournaments. Registration may be completed by parents/guardians or with their consent. Parents wishing to update or remove information regarding a minor can contact the data controller.

## 8. Your Rights

As a data subject, you have the right to:

* **Access:** Request a copy of the personal data held about you.
* **Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate data.
* **Erasure:** Request deletion of private contact information (email and phone).
* **Restriction & Objection:** Object to or request restriction of data processing.
* **Data Portability:** Request an export of personal data you provided.

To exercise your rights, email [amund.fylling@puck.no](mailto:amund.fylling@puck.no). You also have the right to file a complaint with the Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).
